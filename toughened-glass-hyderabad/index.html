Do you supply toughened glass for building projects?

Yes. We supply cut-to-size toughened glass for apartment, villa and commercial projects, with installation if required.

Why must toughened glass be cut before toughening?

Toughened glass cannot be cut or drilled after toughening, so exact sizes, holes and cut-outs are finalised from site measurement first.
